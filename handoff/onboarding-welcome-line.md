# Onboarding: the Welcome's new line

12 November 2026 · Kate Hester

One line on the Welcome changes, to words from Eric's why statement. The heading, the serif line, the button and the layout stay as they are. The full page is in the Onboarding spec, section 3.1.

## Before and now

| | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Line | "Your private career advisor that doesn't just show you the way — it works for you and with you to get you there." | "Part coach, part advisor, part confidant. Private to you, and built around where you want to go." |
| Heading | Somewhere to work on what comes next. | Unchanged |
| Serif line | From "I'd like to be" to "I'm going to be." | Unchanged |
| Words on the page | 39 | 33, within the 40-word rule |

## Where it shows

- **Phone and tablet:** the cover, under the heading.
- **Web:** the split Welcome's sheet, under the heading "From 'I'd like to be' to 'I'm going to be.'"
- **Login is not changed.** Its line is its own.
