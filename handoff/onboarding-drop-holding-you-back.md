# Onboarding: "What's holding you back?" is dropped

12 November 2026 · Kate Hester

The question asked before the plan on the influence path is removed. The full rules are in the Onboarding spec, sections 3.6 and 5.2, and decision 28.

## Before and now

| | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who saw it | Anyone whose direction was "Take on a bigger leadership role" or "Carry more weight where I am" | No one |
| Where | After narrowing, before Choose your plan | — |
| The question | What's holding you back? (I'm not in the room · I'm in the room but not heard · Too junior on paper · Politics · Decisions are made before the meeting; four more) | — |
| Its effect | Shaped the reason on ExecEngine's pick; "Too junior on paper" sent the pick to Step Up | Lead Where You Are is ExecEngine's pick for these directions, with a reason from where she is today and her direction |

## Rules

- **The influence path** now goes: Where are you today → Direction → narrowing → Choose your plan → reflections and the two "Making it yours" questions (Where do you want more say? Who do you most need on side?).
- **Other paths keep their question before the plan:** What's in the way? (a bigger role) and What's making you want a change? (a move within the company). A coming moment has none, as before.
