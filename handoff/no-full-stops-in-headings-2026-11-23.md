# No full stops in headings

23 November 2026 · Kate Hester

Headings never end in a full stop. Question marks stay ("What should we call the cohort?"), and body copy, ledes and notes keep their full stops. The words are otherwise unchanged.

## Build rule

A heading (h1 to h6, or text styled as one, such as the Playbook's "Your statement changed") ends without a full stop. It may end in a question mark.
