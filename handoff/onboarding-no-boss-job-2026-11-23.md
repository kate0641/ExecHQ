# Onboarding: no "My boss's job"

23 November 2026 · Kate Hester

"My boss's job" is gone from **Which role are you going for?**, the first question on the Step Up path. Naming the boss's job as the goal is too confrontational for the product's voice.

## Before and now

| | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Options shown | My boss's job · One level up, in my area · Running a whole function · A seat on the exec team · A role that doesn't exist yet | One level up, in my area · Running a whole function · A seat on the exec team · A role that doesn't exist yet · A bigger role in another part of the business |
| Held back (More options) | A bigger role in another part of the business · General manager of a business · A bigger P&L · A company-wide role | General manager of a business · A bigger P&L · A company-wide role |

"A bigger role in another part of the business" moves up from More to take the empty place, so the question still shows five. It fits growing inside the company rather than leaving it, and its read-back line is unchanged.

The removed option's read-back line ("You're going for your boss's job, so the case is about being the obvious successor."), its first-person line in the story draft and its goal phrase in the plan go with it. The question stays single choice.
