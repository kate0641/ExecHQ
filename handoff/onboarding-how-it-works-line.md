# Onboarding: What ExecEngine is says how it works

17 November 2026 · Kate Hester

What ExecEngine is says what ExecEngine gives her, but not how it gets there. A line under the heading now says it, so she knows why the questions come before she meets them. The full rules are in the Onboarding spec, section 3.2 and decision 45.

## What changed

| Where | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What ExecEngine is, under the heading | (no line) | ExecEngine gathers insight from you to create a customized path forward. |
| The page's word count | 40 | 52, over the 40-word rule (spec 4.10) |

## How it behaves

- The line is there from the first frame, with the heading. It is not part of the build-in; the three statements, the Playbook and privacy rise in at the same moments as before.
- It is the page's ordinary line, styled as every onboarding page's line under its heading.
