# Onboarding: an internal interview

16 November 2026 · Kate Hester

From the 16 November onboarding review with Eric: "An interview for a role here" could read as a job search outside the company, and ExecEngine keeps people within their company. The full rules are in the Onboarding spec, decision 41.

## What changed

| Where | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What's coming up? (Make the Move), last answer | An interview for a role here | An internal interview |
| Its line once picked | You have an interview for a role here coming up, where the first ten minutes decide the rest. | You have an internal interview coming up, where the first ten minutes decide the rest. |
| Its goal, on Your plan | Your path to your interview | Your path to your internal interview |
| Choose your plan (after I can't decide), label | Your plan | Your first step, as on Where do you want to start? |
