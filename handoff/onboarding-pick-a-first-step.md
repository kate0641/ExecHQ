# Onboarding: pick a first step

16 November 2026 · Kate Hester

From the 16 November onboarding review with Eric: choosing one of five plans read as committing to a single path, when an executive wants several. The page now says the plan she picks is where she begins, and that she can change it. The full rules are in the Onboarding spec, section 3.4b and decision 39.

## What changed

| Part | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label | Your plan | Your first step |
| Heading | Where do you want to start? | Unchanged |
| Line | Pick the one that sounds most like you. The next few questions make it yours. | Everyone's path starts somewhere. Pick one to begin with. You can change it at any time. |

Nothing else on the page changes: the five plans, opening a plan, Pick this if, Use this plan and I can't decide at the foot are as before. Behind the scenes she still chooses one plan.

"Change it at any time" is kept by Change your direction on the Plan page.
